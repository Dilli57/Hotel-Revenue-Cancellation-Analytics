# 🏨 Hotel Revenue & Cancellation Analytics Dashboard

## Executive Summary

Built an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution using SQL, Python, and Power BI to analyze **119K hotel bookings, 235K guests, and $42.7M revenue**. This project uncovers revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, booking demand trends, and cancellation risks while providing actionable business recommendations for hotel management.

---

## Business Problem

Hotel operators face multiple challenges including revenue fluctuations, high cancellation rates, low customer retention, and dependency on limited booking channels. This project was designed to help stakeholders understand:

* What drives hotel revenue?
* Which booking channels perform best?
* Why do customers cancel reservations?
* Which customer segments are most valuable?
* How can revenue loss be reduced?

# Dashboard Pages

## 1. Executive Overview

### KPIs

* Total Revenue: $42.72M
* Total Bookings: 119K
* Repeat Customer Rate: 3.19%
* Average ADR: $101.83
* Cancellation Rate: 37.04%

### Key Insights

* Revenue peaked in 2016 before stabilizing.
* City Hotels generated the highest revenue contribution.
* Booking demand increased significantly during summer months.
* Cancellation rates remained a major operational challenge.

---

## 2. Booking Intelligence

### KPIs

* Total Bookings
* Average Stay Duration
* Average Lead Time
* Weekend Stay %

### Key Insights

* August recorded the highest booking demand.
* City Hotels contributed nearly 66% of bookings.
* Guests booked an average of 104 days before arrival.
* Online Travel Agencies generated the highest booking volume.

### Recommendations

* Increase promotions during peak demand periods.
* Strengthen high-performing booking channels.
* Implement early-booking pricing strategies.

---

## 3. Revenue Analytics

### KPIs

* Total Revenue
* Average ADR
* Revenue Per Booking
* Revenue Per Night

### Key Insights

* August generated the highest monthly revenue ($7.9M).
* City Hotels contributed approximately 60% of total revenue.
* Online Travel Agencies generated the largest revenue share.
* Revenue increased steadily through August before declining.

### Recommendations

* Implement dynamic pricing during peak demand.
* Focus investment on high-performing acquisition channels.
* Improve demand forecasting accuracy.

---

## 4. Cancellation Risk Analytics

### KPIs

* Cancellation Rate
* Cancelled Bookings
* Average Lead Time (Cancelled)
* Average ADR (Cancelled)

### Key Insights

* Long lead-time bookings exhibited the highest cancellation risk.
* Non-refundable deposits significantly reduced cancellations.
* Transient customers accounted for most cancellations.
* Cancellation activity increased during peak demand periods.

### Recommendations

* Encourage non-refundable booking options.
* Improve booking confirmation workflows.
* Apply stricter cancellation policies for high-risk reservations.

---

## 5. Advanced Risk Analytics

### Key Influencers

* Deposit Type
* Lead Time
* ADR
* Market Segment

### Findings

* Deposit type is the strongest predictor of cancellation behavior.
* Lead times above 180 days significantly increase cancellation probability.
* Market segment and ADR strongly influence cancellation risk.
* High-risk reservations can be identified proactively.

### Recommendations

* Monitor high-risk bookings.
* Strengthen customer communication before arrival.
* Promote partial or non-refundable deposits.

---

## 6. Customer Analytics

### KPIs

* Total Guests: 235K
* Repeat Guests: 3,810
* Countries Served: 178
* Average Guests per Booking: 1.97

### Key Insights

* Transient customers dominate the customer base.
* Repeat customer rates remain relatively low.
* Demand is concentrated among a few key countries.
* Adult travelers account for the majority of guest volume.

### Recommendations

* Launch loyalty programs.
* Improve repeat customer engagement.
* Expand into emerging geographic markets.
